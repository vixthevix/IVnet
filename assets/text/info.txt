IVnet is a program that allows you to 
connect your Generation IV Pokemon 
games to custom fan made servers, 
all from your Linux desktop.

By using a Network Interface Device
(preferably a USB WiFi dongle) on your
desktop, your games can connect
to the Internet, all in a couple of clicks!


I hope you enjoy using IVnet!
